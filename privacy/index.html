Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information itemsss collects, why we use it, and how we handle it when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

  • We collect only the information needed to provide and maintain itemsss, including:
  • Account information, such as your email address and profile name.
  • Inventory information you choose to save, such as item names, photos, quantities, notes, categories, and storage locations.
  • Account and subscription information needed to determine your Free, Trial, or Pro access.
  • Technical information required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain your itemsss account and inventory.
  • Save, sync, search, and organize your belongings.
  • Provide features such as storage locations, reminders, QR codes, and subscription access.
  • Keep the app secure and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback you choose to send us.

3. How We Protect Your Data

We use authentication, access controls, and protected storage to help keep your account and inventory data private and accessible only to authorized users.

No system can guarantee absolute security, but we design itemsss to limit access to your information and protect it from unauthorized use.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may use trusted service providers to operate essential parts of itemsss, such as authentication, data storage, subscriptions, and app distribution. These services may process information only as needed to provide their functions.

5. Your Choices & Rights

You can view and update supported profile information from your Account.

You can also delete your itemsss account. Account deletion permanently removes your account and associated user-owned app data, subject to limited information we may need to retain for security, fraud prevention, legal, or service-integrity purposes.

Your registered account email cannot currently be changed from within itemsss.

Data Usage & Retention

Last updated: November 2026

itemsss stores information only for as long as it is needed to provide the app, maintain account security, or meet applicable requirements.

Account & Profile

Your account and supported profile information are kept while your account remains active.

If you delete your account, your user-owned account data is scheduled for permanent removal according to the account deletion process.

Inventory & Photos

Items, photos, notes, categories, quantities, and storage locations you save remain associated with your account until you remove them or delete your account.

Moving from Pro to Free does not automatically delete existing inventory or photos that exceed Free plan limits. Free plan limits apply to new activity after downgrade.

Notifications

Notification Inbox messages are kept for up to 30 days and are automatically removed after the retention period.

Feedback

Feedback you send us is kept for up to 90 days and then automatically deleted.

Subscription & Trial Information

itemsss keeps the information needed to determine your current plan, Trial eligibility, and subscription access.

Some limited account or identity information may be retained after account deletion when necessary to prevent Trial abuse, maintain service integrity, meet legal obligations, or protect the service.

Account Deletion

Deleting your account permanently removes user-owned itemsss data associated with that account and cannot be undone.

If you have an active Pro subscription with auto-renewal enabled, you must manage your subscription before account deletion can proceed.

Service Providers

Some information may be processed by service providers that help itemsss operate features such as authentication, secure data storage, subscriptions, and app distribution. Their own privacy policies may also apply to information they process.
