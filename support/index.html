Support

Need help with itemsss? Email us at support@itemsss.app. We aim to reply within a few days.

Delete your account

  1. Open itemsss and go to Account → Delete Account.
  2. If you have Pro with auto-renewal on, first cancel it in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription.
  3. Tap Confirm. Your account, items and photos are deleted right away and cannot be recovered.

Can't open the app? Email support@itemsss.app from the email address of your account and we'll delete your account and data within 30 days.

Restore a purchase

Go to Account → Billing Information → Restore.
